01 / Listens

It never stops listening.

A wireless mic feeds an ESP32 around the clock. A noise gate throws away silence, fans and background chatter, so only speech reaches 0G. Say “Hey Atlas” and it acts. Otherwise it just remembers.

02 / Remembers

It remembers the room.

Summaries

It closes a conversation after a pause and writes the summary itself. Ask later, “what did we decide about the quota?”

Nudges

It hears what you promised and reminds you ten minutes later. It never invents a commitment.

“You said you’d follow up with Rohan.”

Transcripts

Ask for the full transcript or an export. Conversations are hashed and stored on 0G, so they can be verified.

05 / Built on 0G

0G is the brain.

Compute

Whisper turns speech into text. A language model turns it into an intent and actions. Every call has a request id.

Storage

Each decision is saved as an episode and uploaded to 0G Storage with its hash.

Chain

A contract on 0G Galileo anchors every episode.
